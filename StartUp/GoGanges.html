GoGanges

Pinkparrot LLP
GoGanges.com is an advanced web-based platform to book treatments/ wellness holidays at curated Wellness Retreats. With Yoga, Ayurveda, Naturopathy, you can de-stress, lose weight or get treatment for incurable diseases.

Founding Members

Nikhil Sethi
Co-founder
Aditi Bansal
Co-founder

Team Members

  • 2 founders
  • 2 interns

Target Market

  • Stressed professionals
  • 55-year old housewife with glaucoma or a 35-year old businessman with low back pain

Sector

  • Health-Tech

Problem Statement

Alternative Treatments like Ayurveda or Yoga exist but we can’t find the right Wellness Retreat to trust. We are solving this problem by providing a one-stop-shop of curated wellness treatments and retreats in India.

Value Proposition

Mass Appeal | Targeting 90% of revenue from medical treatments at NABH and insurance-approved retreats.

Current Stage

  • Early traction

Type of Business

  • Platform-based

Products / Services

Advanced web-based platform to book treatments/ wellness holidays at curated Wellness Retreats with yoga, Ayurveda, naturopathy.

Key Highlights

  • Achieved a revenue of INR 70 lacs in FY 2018-19
  • Won "Best Wellness Travel Company of the year" from lux-review.com
