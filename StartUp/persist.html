Persist Energy

Deusexvolt Electric Pvt. Ltd.
Persist is making transportation affordable to the micro entrepreneurs of India by making EV technology affordable and suitable for the infrastructure of India through localization, indigenous platform development, fostering supporting ecosystem and flexible battery ownership.

Problem Statement

Small business owners spend most of their income on fuel and make a great use case to catalyze the adoption of EVs. The problem is that the EVs currently being sold are either built for urban commuters or are way too poor in quality to support the requirements of these customer segments. On the top of that EVs are typically are 30% more expensive than ICE vehicles of same performance.

Value Proposition

A real, practical and tailored option for transportation needs of MSMEs so that they may reduce their logistics expenses and scale faster.

Products / Services

Building EVs designed to meet the requirements of growing MSMEs in India, and providing the ecosystem necessary for MSMEs to adopt EVs with innovative business models.
